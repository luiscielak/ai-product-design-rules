Annotations
- A1: Stage index + name. Back to run-overview.
- A2: Artifact body. For code/html stages this is a scroll region.
- A3: Empty if the agent has not written files (FR-004).
- A4: Loading.
- A5: IO / parse error.
- A6: Approved stages omit decision buttons (FR-007).

Flow hooks
- Next: run-overview (approve) or revision-request.
- Prev: run-overview.
- If this is stage 6 and approved: run-complete.
